Visualisation proposal · Animation & infographics

A second city,
46 metres up.

Raumpark Vienna adds an elevated public landscape above the existing block grid. We propose to tell it as one story that zooms from Vienna's heat map down to a single window at Westbahnhof and up onto the deck: sun, wind, everyday light and the new uses, in animation and infographics.

02

Storyboard

Package 1: a proposed three-minute story in ten shots, from the whole city down to one window, then up onto the deck: each use in its own shot, and a diagram of greenery and solar income. The sketches are schematic: they show the idea of each shot, not its final look.

03

Timetable

From the kick-off on 28 October 2026 to the end of February 2027, in two packages. Package 1 produces a three-minute film in ten shots (S1–S10) and is reviewed in mid-January. After that review we propose Package 2: further animations, defined together.
