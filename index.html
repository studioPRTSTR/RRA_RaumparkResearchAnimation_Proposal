Timetable

From the kick-off on 28 October 2026 to the end of February 2027, in two packages. Package 1 starts with three weeks of pre-production (Vienna data, the 3D model of the area, storyboard and look), animates the ten shots (S1–S10) of a three-minute film by mid-December, renders them over the holidays and is reviewed in the first week of January. After that review we propose Package 2: further animations, defined together.
